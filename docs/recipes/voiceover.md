# Voiceover videos

A voiceover video is told only in your voice, over photos and clips you already have: a project
you built, a trip, a day. Nobody is on camera.

## What you need

- The Yap Studio workspace, set up (`python3 setup.py` reports ready).
- Your media: a Photos album, or any folder of photos and clips.
- For a Photos album: a Mac and osxphotos (`uv tool install osxphotos`). Without it, export the
  album yourself: in Photos, select the album's items, then File > Export > Export Items. That
  export keeps your edits.
- A way to record your voice. Voice Memos on your phone is enough.
- For generated music or sound effects: an ElevenLabs account. Sign up at
  https://littlemight.com/recommend/elevenlabs (affiliate link).

## The steps

1. Tell the agent: "Make a voiceover video from my Kitchen album." Use your album's name, or
   point it to a folder. On your first video, the agent saves this as your usual kind of video
   and asks where it may get B-roll if you have not said.
2. The agent copies the album into a folder and looks at every photo and clip. It writes a short
   log of what the footage shows, in the order you shot it.
3. It writes a short script in your voice, hook first, using only what the footage shows and
   what you tell it. You approve or edit the script.
4. You record the script and drop the memo in `inbox/`. Record a second memo for any line you
   want to redo.
5. The agent builds the story from your recording and trims filler words and long pauses. You
   listen to it on your phone. The picture is black at this point; this review is for the words.
6. The agent covers every second with your photos and clips, in the order they happened. Only
   the opening hook and a before and after may jump ahead.
7. It adds music if you want it, renders captions, and sends you the whole video. You comment on
   your phone. It replies to each note and sends a new version until you approve one.

A long video can take more than 30 minutes to render. The render keeps going in the background.

## Recording tips

- Hold the phone 8 to 10 inches from your mouth and a little to the side. Closer makes your
  voice boomy.
- Record in a quiet room with soft things around you, such as a sofa or curtains.
- Read the whole script once. If you stumble, pause and say the line again. The agent keeps the
  best read.
- Fix a line later with a short pickup memo. Drop it in `inbox/` and say which line it replaces.

## What the agent does

- It never changes your Photos library or your files. It works on copies.
- It uses your edited version of anything you edited in Photos, such as a crop or a trim, and the
  original of everything else.
- If some of the album is not downloaded to your Mac, it tells you before the export starts.
  Photos downloads those items during the export, and macOS may ask for permission. Stay at the
  Mac to click Allow, or export the album yourself.
- It uses one shot per moment, so the same scene does not repeat.
- It asks early whether children, friends, or anyone else may appear in the video.
- It ends on a shot it has not used earlier in the video.
- If a graphic or diagram fits a clear point in your script, it puts it there and tells you.
- It asks before spending money, such as ElevenLabs music.

## What you review

- The script, before you record.
- The story by ear, before any picture goes in.
- The whole finished video, round after round, until you approve a version.
